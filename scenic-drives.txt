With **Santa Maria near Dobbiaco/Toblach** as your likely **East base**, and **Naz-Sciaves / Brixen / Kastelruth / Ortisei** as your **West-base options**, the scenic-drive ROI shifts like this:

* **Val di Funes** gets **better** if you stay in **Brixen or Naz-Sciaves**
* **Sellaronda** stays best only if you stay in **Ortisei**; it becomes less “easy win” from **Brixen/Naz**
* **Great Dolomites Road** is now best as a **West base → Dobbiaco transfer route**, not as an Ortisei → Cortina route
* **Passo Giau** drops a bit because **Dobbiaco is farther from Giau than Cortina is**
* **Tre Cime / Misurina approach** becomes much more useful because **Dobbiaco is close to Misurina and the Tre Cime access corridor**. Dobbiaco to Misurina is about **20.6 km / 20–25 minutes**, Dobbiaco to Cortina is about **32–33 km / ~30–50 minutes**, and Toblach to Giau is about **48 km / ~45 minutes**. Brixen to Santa Maddalena is about **18.9 km / ~19 minutes**, Brixen to Ortisei about **29.5 km / ~1 hour by bus / much shorter by car**, and Brixen to Kastelruth about **25 km / ~24 minutes**. ([Rome2Rio][1])

## Updated final scenic-drive ranking for your likely bases

1. **Val di Funes valley drive** — best if you stay **Brixen or Naz-Sciaves**
2. **Great Dolomites Road practical transfer to Dobbiaco** — best on your **West → East hotel move**
3. **Sellaronda loop** — best only if you stay **Ortisei**; still good from **Kastelruth**, less ideal from **Brixen/Naz**
4. **Tre Cime / Misurina approach mini-drive** — now much more practical from **Dobbiaco**
5. **Passo Giau loop** — still beautiful, but no longer the first East-side backup from your actual East base
